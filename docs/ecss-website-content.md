## SECTION 9 — FAQ (Suggested)

**Headline:**
Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q: How is my monthly price calculated?**
Your fee is based on your annual revenue. ECSS starts at $200/month for businesses earning up to $100,000 per year, with $75 added for every additional $50,000 in revenue — capped permanently at $2,000/month regardless of how large you grow.

**Q: What if I exceed the revenue threshold mid-year?**
Your pricing is reviewed and adjusted on an agreed schedule. We'll let you know in advance before any change is made to your subscription.

**Q: Do I need QuickBooks before signing up?**
No. QuickBooks Online setup and configuration is included in your ECSS subscription. We set it up from scratch, properly structured for your business.

**Q: What happens if I receive more than 2 government agency notices in a month?**
The base plan covers up to 2 federal or state agency notices per month. Any additional notice is handled at $100 per notice — we'll always notify you before proceeding.

**Q: Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?**
ECSS is a subscription service structured around your business stage. Specific terms are outlined in your service agreement. Contact us to discuss what commitment structure works for you.

**Q: Do you work with businesses outside of Maryland?**
We work with businesses across the United States. While we're headquartered in Columbia, MD, our services are not limited by geography.
